Scarlet Rox is a full-service salon on East Center Street in Moab — hair, lashes and nails, with the kind of attention only a small team can give.

The philosophy

Moab deserves a great salon.

Between the river trips and the red rock, people here live outdoors — and still want to look sharp doing it. This salon was built for that life: colour that survives the sun, lashes that survive the river, nails that survive the trailhead.

Everything runs on appointments, every first visit starts with a consultation, and nobody is ever rushed out of the chair.
